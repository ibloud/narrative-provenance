## Labels

- **Verified:** directly supported by cited evidence.
- **Creator-stated:** stated by the creator or project owner.
- **Third-party claim:** stated by a named external source.
- **Interpretation:** analysis of meaning or function.
- **Inference:** conclusion drawn from listed evidence.
- **Unresolved:** evidence is missing, inaccessible, ambiguous, or conflicting.
