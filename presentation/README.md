# Make This Presentation Your Own

[`openai-event.md`](openai-event.md) is an editable presentation source. It is written as a sequence of slides separated by `---`, so it can be used as Markdown, adapted to presentation software, or rendered with a Markdown slide tool.

## Recommended order

1. Replace the bracketed community and example placeholders.
2. Keep the evidence labels and safety boundaries intact unless your adaptation documents why they changed.
3. Use a fictional, consensual, public-domain, or safely redacted example.
4. Demonstrate capture, classification, connection, pause, and resume.
5. Add image descriptions and speaker notes for any new visuals.
6. Test the story with someone unfamiliar with the project.
7. Credit the method, your adaptation, and any contributors separately.

## Accessibility checklist

- One idea per slide.
- Minimum 24-point body text when exported to slides.
- High contrast and no meaning conveyed by color alone.
- Plain-language labels remain visible.
- Describe visuals aloud and provide alt text in shared versions.
- Avoid auto-playing animation and rapid transitions.
- Share an accessible text version alongside the deck.
- End with one concrete way to participate.

The presentation is a starting structure, not an official OpenAI template or an indication of OpenAI affiliation or endorsement.
