# A Beginning for the Collective

This project begins from a simple belief: creative people should not have to become archivists, investigators, or database designers before their work can be understood.

The collective's job is not to decide whose story is true. It is to make evidence easier to preserve, uncertainty safer to admit, interpretation easier to identify, and interrupted work easier to resume.

## What we are building together

1. A platform-neutral provenance method anyone can read and adapt.
2. Small templates that work before any software is installed.
3. Accessible implementations, beginning with the Narrative Provenance Obsidian plugin and the `creative-provenance-workflow` ChatGPT skill.
4. Teaching materials people can remake for their own communities.
5. A shared body of privacy-preserving examples and unresolved design questions.

## Collective principles

- Preserve before reorganizing.
- Capture before classification.
- One decision and one next action at a time.
- `Unknown` and `return later` are valid outcomes.
- Interpretation is valuable when it is labeled.
- Rights, consent, affiliation, and endorsement are separate questions.
- The person doing the work controls what is shared.
- Accessibility includes cognitive load, recovery after interruption, mobile use, and emotional safety.
- Examples must be consensual, fictional, public-domain, or safely redacted.
- No one must publish private evidence to prove that a problem exists.

## Questions the collective can help resolve

- What is the smallest record that still permits a person to return later?
- Which labels are clearest across writing, music, archives, journalism, and research?
- How should tools show conflicting evidence without creating alarm or false certainty?
- How can relationship maps remain useful without implying affiliation or causation?
- Which prompts reduce cognitive load without taking control away from the creator?
- How should exports remain portable across Markdown, JSON, CSV, and other tools?
- What accessibility testing should every implementation pass?
- How can communities translate the method without flattening culturally specific authorship practices?

## Ways to participate

- Test one template with fictional or non-sensitive material.
- Describe one place where the workflow became confusing.
- Propose clearer language for one label.
- Add an accessible example for a new creative or research context.
- Test keyboard, screen-reader, mobile, reduced-motion, or interruption recovery behavior.
- Implement an adapter while preserving the canonical evidence distinctions.

Begin with the [workflow](WORKFLOW.md), review the [neurodivergent design principles](docs/neurodivergent-design.md), then use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to propose a focused change.
